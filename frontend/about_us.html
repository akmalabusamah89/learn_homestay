About InappRest

Meet InappRest, the premier platform redefining homestay and property management. Named after the concepts of lodging (Inap), technology (App), and relaxation (Rest), our secure system empowers property owners and administrators to effortlessly handle bookings and master operational controls all in one place.

Our Core Features

Property Management

Easily manage your homestays, update details, and oversee availability in real-time.

Secure Bookings

Track guest reservations, check-in and check-out dates, and payment statuses seamlessly.

Role-Based Security

Robust administrative controls ensuring platform safety with dedicated owner and admin portals.
